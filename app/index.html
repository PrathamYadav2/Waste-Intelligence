      End-to-End Waste Recycling Workflow

      Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) based on Solid Waste Management (SWM) 2016 Rules and circular economy best practices.

      1
      Source Segregation
      Segregate at origin into 3 distinct color-coded bins:
      • Green Bin: Wet / Biodegradable
      • Blue Bin: Dry Recyclables
      • Red/Black: Domestic Hazardous
      2
      Citizen Pre-Cleaning
      Divert contamination:
      • Rinse milk pouches & food cans
      • Flatten cardboard cartons to save 70% bin volume
      • Keep dry paper away from oil/moisture
      3
      Decentralized MRF
      Primary sorting at Material Recovery Facility:
      • Trommel screens for size separation
      • Magnetic cross-belt separators for ferrous metals
      • Baling machines for transport efficiency
      4
      Industrial Processing
      Specialized recovery pipelines:
      • Plastics: Shredded, washed, melted into PCR granules
      • Organics: Biomethanation into CNG & bio-fertilizer
      • Glass/Metals: Smelted into reusable ingots
      5
      Circular Economy
      Secondary raw materials sent directly to certified manufacturers to eliminate landfill dependency and reduce carbon emissions.
      🧴 Plastic & Dry Waste Detailed Protocol
      Blue Stream

      1. PET Bottles: Remove bottle cap (HDPE), crush body, transport to mechanical shredder. Flakes washed at 85°C caustic bath to remove adhesives.

      2. Cardboard & Kraft Paper: Hydrapulped in water to separate fibers, screened through centrifugal cleaners, and pressed into heavy recycled paperboard sheets.

      3. Aluminium & Cans: De-coated and melted at 660°C. Requires 95% less energy than refining primary bauxite ore.

      🌿 Wet Waste & Biomethanation Protocol
      Green Stream

      1. Food Organics: Processed via anaerobic digestion in decentralized biomethanation digesters. 1 tonne wet waste generates ~80-100 m³ biogas.

      2. Garden & Horticultural Waste: Chipped and used as bulking agent in aerated windrow composting. Reaches 55°C thermophilic phase to destroy pathogens.

      3. Output Utilization: Certified compost enriched with nitrogen and enriched bio-gas piped for clean cooking fuel.
